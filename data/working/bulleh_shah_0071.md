# Original

ساوَن

ساوَن سوہے مِینھ گلا، گھَٹ سوہے کُتار
تُھور تُھور عِنایت وَستے پیہا کرے پُکار

سوہن ملھاراں سارے ساوَن
دُوتی دُکھ لگّے اُٹھ جاوَن
بِنینگر کھیڈن، گڈیاں گاوَن
مَیں گھر رنگ رنگیلے آوَن
آساں پُنّیاں

میریاں آساں رَبّ پُچّائیاں
سِتیاں دین مُبارک آئیاں
مَیں تاں اُن سنگ اکھیاں لائیاں
شاہ عِنایت رنگ لگائیاں
آساں پُنّیاں

# Literal Translation

RAIN

Rain on time brings joy to all
is what the koels call.

          Sweet sounds the flute,
          boys and girls are at play,
          now my sighs bear fruit,
          love has come to stay.

                    Barriers fall.

          Friends hurry to my side
          with 'Lucky, lucky you.'
          My mentor, my guide
          made my dreams come true.

                    Love is all.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.209. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

Rain

Lovely is the rain-season, the clouds and the drizzle; the thunder-cloud is fair as it gathers,
and drop by drop the grace pours down — the pied cuckoo cries out for it.

Beautiful are the malhaar songs all through the rains;
the sorrow that once made me rise and leave has fled.
Girl-companions play, they sing at their spinning-wheels,
and to my house the many-hued colours have come —
my hopes are fulfilled.

God has heard my prayers;
my friends have come with the blessings of the faith.
As for me, I fixed my eyes upon Him:
Shah Inayat has coloured me with his hue —
my hopes are fulfilled.
