# Original

جو رنگ رنگیا گوہڑا رنگیا

جو رنگ رنگیا گوہڑا رنگیا، مرشد والی لالی او یار
دُرِ معانی کی دھوم مچی ہے، نیناں توں گھونڈ اُٹھالیں او یار
زُلف سیاہ وچ ہو یدِ بَیضا، اوہ چمکار دِکھالیں او یار

چُپ کو چُپ عُظمیٰ ہوئیاں، لائیاں دی لَج پالیں او یار
موتوا قبل اَن تموتوا، موئی نوں پھیر جوالیں او یار

اَدکھا جھیڑا عشق دے والا، سنبھل کے پَیر پُکالیں او یار
ہر شے اندر توں آپے ہیں، آپے دیکھ دِکھالیں او یار

بُلھیا! شوہ گھر میرے آیا، کر کر ناچ دِکھالیں او یار
جو رنگ رنگیا گوہڑا رنگیا، مرشد والی لالی او یار

# Literal Translation

Whatever you touch takes on your hue.

Your eyes have caused a stir, unveil yourself
like the cloud-swept moon when it breaks through.

This dumbness is not a pose. For those who die
before death can strike them, their life renew.

Too many obstacles! Now take my hand
and help me over them as if I flew.

Dance, Bulleh, dance, your love has come to you.
Whatever you touch takes on your hue.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.85. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training. Rafat renders the "die before you die" hadith and the immanence couplet freely._

# AI Translation

Whatever hue was dyed, it was dyed deep and fast — the crimson of the murshid, O friend.

The pearl of meaning has raised a great clamour; lift the veil from your eyes, O friend.
In your black tresses be the radiant hand of Moses — show me that flash of light, O friend.

[Silence itself has become the highest silence]; keep faith with those who gave themselves to you, O friend.
"Die before you die" — then breathe life once more into the one who has died, O friend.

The quarrel of love is half-hidden; set your feet down with care, O friend.
You are Yourself within every thing; unveil Yourself and let Yourself be seen, O friend.

Bulleh — the Beloved has come to my house; dance and dance and show it, O friend!
Whatever hue was dyed, it was dyed deep and fast — the crimson of the murshid, O friend.
