# Original

مِل لئو سہیلڑیو

مِل لئو سہیلڑیو، میری رِلج گہیلڑیو، مَیں ساہُوریاں گھر جانا
تُساں بھی ہوسی اللہ بھانا، مَیں ساہُوریاں گھر جانا
اماں بابل داج جو دِتّا: اِک چولی اِک چُنّی
داج تِنہاں دا دیکھ کے تُن ہَیں، جنہوں بھر بھر رَتّی
اِک وچھوڑا سِیاں دا، جو ویں ڈاروں کُونج وِچھُنّی

رنگ برنگے سُول اپَنّے، جانڈے چیر نیں جی نوں
ایتھوں دے دُکھ نال بھلاواں، اَگلے سونہاں کیہنوں
سسّت زِناماں دیوَن طعنے، بڑی نموشی مَینوں

ایتھے سانوں رہِن نہ مِلدا، جانا وار و واری
چنگے چنگیرے پکڑ منگاسے، مَیں کمَیدی پہلائی
جِس گُنّی پلّے بول سَوتّے، سوئی ہووے نوں پیاری
مِل لئو سہیلڑیو، میری رِلج گہیلڑیو، مَیں ساہُوریاں گھر جانا
تُساں بھی ہوسی اللہ بھانا، مَیں ساہُوریاں گھر جانا

# Literal Translation

Gather round me, friends, my stay here is done.
I'm off to the house where you'll join me soon.

What was my dowry?  A stove and a shawl.
When I think of it now the tear-drops fall.
Like a lost crane I'll roam when I leave you all.

So many arrows of pain that struck home
deck the body, but what of those to come?
They will sneer at me there; this is my doom.

All must go, of remission not a word.
Who am I, when my betters were not spared?
Only the righteous will get bed and board.

Gather round me, friends, my stay here is done.
I'm off to the house where you'll join me soon.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.157. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

Come, meet me, dear companions

Come and meet me, my beloved friends, my playmates — I am going to my in-laws' house.
This will be God's will for you too; I am going to my in-laws' house.
The dowry my mother and father gave me: one bodice, one veil.
Seeing the dowry others were given, my heart is filled to the brim.
One is the parting of stitched seams — like a crane cut off from her flock.

My own pains, of many colours, tear at and rend my soul.
By the sorrow of this place I am forewarned — to whom shall I make the next vows?
The cheap and worthless ones cast their taunts; a great shame falls on me.

Here we are not granted to stay; each in turn must go.
They will seize and demand the best of the best; I am the least-worthy one.
Whichever bride carries sweet words in her lap, she alone is dear.
Come and meet me, my beloved friends, my playmates — I am going to my in-laws' house.
This will be God's will for you too; I am going to my in-laws' house.
