# Original

مَینوں لگڑا عشق

مَینوں لگڑا عشق اَولڑا
اوّل دا، روزِ اَزل دا
وِچ کڑاہی تِل تِل پا دے
تِلیاں نُوں چا تلدا

مویاں نُوں ایہ وَل وَل مارے
دِلیاں نُوں چا دلدا

کیا جاناں کوئی چنگ سکھی اے
نِت سُول کلیجے سلّ دا

بُلّھا! شوہ دا نِیوں اَنوکھا
اوہ نہیں رلایاں رلدا
مَینوں لگڑا عشق اَولڑا
اوّل دا، روزِ اَزل دا

# Literal Translation

From the first moment love
has put me to the proof.

He tossed me in the frying-pan,
and tosses me over again.

Not satisfied with my death,
he kills with every breath.

One ember on the body
spread till it engulfed me.

Bulleh, love will not settle
for less, your most is little.

From the first moment love
has put me to the proof.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.171. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

Love has seized me — a strange, first love,
love from the beginning, from the day of eternity.

It throws me into the cauldron bit by bit,
frying the sesame-seeds one by one.

Again and again it strikes down the already-slain,
grinding the hearts to a pulp.

How should I know? Some friend must have learned this game —
a thorn forever pierces my breast.

Bulleh! the Beloved's love is a wondrous thing;
He does not mingle with just anyone who tries.
Love has seized me — a strange, first love,
love from the beginning, from the day of eternity.
