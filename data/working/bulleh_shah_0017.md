# Original

بُلھے نوں سمجھاون آئیاں

بُلھے نوں سمجھاون آئیاں بھیناں تے بھرجائیاں

مَن لے بُلھیا ساڈا کہنا، چھڈ دے پلّا رائیاں
آلِ نبی اولادِ علی نوں، توں کیوں لیکاں لائیاں؟

جیہڑا سانوں سیّد سدّے، دوزخ ملن سزائیاں
جو کوئی سانوں رائیں آکھے، بہشتی پینگاں پائیاں

رائیں، سائیں سبھنی تھائیں، رب دِیاں بے پروائیاں
سوہنیاں پرے ہٹائیاں، تے کوجھیاں لے گل لائیاں

جے توں لوڑیں باغ بہاراں، چاکر ہو جا رائیاں
بُلھے شاہ دی ذات کیہ پُچھنیں؟ شاکر ہو رضائیاں

# Script Notes

- Script: Shahmukhi (Perso-Arabic)
- Language spans: Punjabi
- Notes: Vision transcription of Rafat poem 16, read from the calligraphic Nastaliq facing page (book p.72). This is the well-known caste kafi in which Bulleh's kinswomen urge him to abandon his Arain (Rai) murshid, Shah Inayat, and he retorts by embracing the "Rai" name. # Transliteration

Bulhe nu samjhaavan aaiyaan

Bulhe nu samjhaavan aaiyaan bhainaan te bharjaaiyaan

Man lai Bulhiya saaḍa kehna, chhaḍ de palla Raaiyaan
Aal-e-Nabi aulaad-e-Ali nu, toon kyun leekaan laaiyaan?

Jehṛa saanu Syed sadde, dozakh milan sazaaiyaan
Jo koi saanu Raeen aakhe, bihishti pinghaan paaiyaan

Raeen, saaeen sabhni thaaeen, rab diyaan be-parwaaiyaan
Sohniyaan pare haṭaaiyaan, te kojhiyaan lai gal laaiyaan

Je toon loṛein baagh bahaaraan, chaakar ho ja Raaiyaan
Bulhe Shah di zaat keh puchhnein? shaakir ho rizaaiyaan

# Literal Translation

Every female from near and afar
turned up to brief Bulleh Shah.
Listen Bulleh, they said, it's a sin
to condemn the prophet's kin.
Those who acknowledge them, you tell,
will get the one-way ticket to hell.
Does sanctity deserve such scorn?

It's God's carelessness where one is born.
When I put the pretty ones in their place
the ugly shoved their noses in my face.
If you seek redemption drop this farce.
My caste?  I've none.  I'm just a man.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.73. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training. Rafat's version is a free modern adaptation that departs considerably from the literal Punjabi (esp. the Syed/Rai caste retort)._

# AI Translation

They came to counsel Bulleh — the sisters and the sisters-in-law:

"Take our advice, Bulleh — let go the Arain's hem!
You, of the Prophet's house, of Ali's line — why have you brought this stain on us?"

Whoever calls me a Sayyid — may the torments of hell be his;
whoever calls me a Rai (Arain) — the swings of paradise are his.

The Master is present in every place — such are the careless ways of God:
He pushes the lovely ones aside and clasps the homely to His breast.

If you long for the garden in bloom, become a servant of the Arain.
Why ask what caste Bulleh Shah is? Be content within His will.
