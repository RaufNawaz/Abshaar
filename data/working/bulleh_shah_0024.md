# Transliteration

Bharosa keh ashnaai da
Ḍar lagda be-parwaai da

# Literal Translation

It is not acquaintance that I dread,
but the indifference to which it may lead.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.89. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._
