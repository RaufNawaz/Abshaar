# Original

جِند کڑکی دے مُنہ آئی

جِند کڑکی دے مُنہ آئی
عشق تُساڈا مینوں دِسدا، پربت کولوں بھارا
اِک گھڑی دے دیکھن کارن، لُٹ لیا سر سارا
محنت ملّے کہ مِلدی ناہیں؟ ڈاڈھے دی اَشنائی
جِند کڑکی دے مُنہ آئی

پاکاں دا ہے وہی وسیلہ، میرے تئیں آپے ہو دَود
جاگدیاں سنگ میرے جاگو، سَونواں نالے سَوندو
جس نے تئیں سنگ پریت لگائی، کیہڑے سُکھ سوائی
جِند کڑکی دے مُنہ آئی

جگ وچ روشن نام تُساڈا، عاشق توں کیوں نَسدے ہو
دستور تو وچ بحل دے، اپنا بھیت نہ دَسدے ہو
اُدھکّے وِچکاروں پھٹ کے، یَیں اُلٹی لَٹکائی
جِند کڑکی دے مُنہ آئی

# Script Notes

- Script: Shahmukhi (Perso-Arabic)
- Language spans: Punjabi
- Notes: Vision transcription of Rafat poem 20, read from the calligraphic Nastaliq facing page (book p.82). The refrain "Jind kaṛki de munh aai" (idiom: life brought to dire straits / to the brink of ruin; Rafat renders it "I'm in a trap"). # Transliteration

Jind kaṛki de munh aai

Jind kaṛki de munh aai
Ishq tusaaḍa mainu disda, parbat kolon bhaara
Ik ghaṛi de dekhan kaaran, luṭ liya sir saara
Mehnat mille ke mildi naaheen? ḍaaḍhe di ashnaai
Jind kaṛki de munh aai

Paakaan da hai vahi vaseela, mere taeen aape ho daud
Jaagdiyaan sang mere jaago, saunvaan naale saundo
Jis ne taeen sang preet lagaai, kehṛe sukh savaai
Jind kaṛki de munh aai

Jag vich roshan naam tusaaḍa, aashiq toon kyun nasde ho
Dastoor to vich bahl de, apna bheet na dasde ho
Udhakke vichkaaron phaṭ ke, yain ulṭi laṭkaai
Jind kaṛki de munh aai

# Literal Translation

I'm in a trap.

Love brought me to this fate;
just one glimpse was the bait.
Did my zeal deserve this blow?

I'm in a trap.

Take pity on my innocence
and give me some recompense.
This death is much too slow.

I'm in a trap.

Within touching distance yet
I cannot reach your heart.
Love me, or let me go.

I'm in a trap.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.83. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training. Rafat compresses each Punjabi stanza into a shorter, freely-worded triplet._

# AI Translation

My life is brought to the brink of ruin.

Your love weighs on me heavier than a mountain;
for the sake of one moment's glimpse it has plundered me whole.
Will my labour be requited, or not — this acquaintance with the Mighty One?
My life is brought to the brink of ruin.

The pure have only this one recourse: that You come running to me yourself.
When I wake, wake beside me; when I sleep, sleep alongside me.
Whoever set her heart on You — what ease did she ever gain?
My life is brought to the brink of ruin.

Your name shines through the world — why then do You flee the lover?
You suspend even love's own custom; You will not tell me Your secret.
[uncertain line — torn apart in the middle, left hanging upside down].
My life is brought to the brink of ruin.
