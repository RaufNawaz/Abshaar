# Original

اُٹھ چلے گواندھوں یار
رَبّا! ہُن کیہ کریے!
اُٹھ چلے، ہُن رہندے ناہیں
ہویا ساتھ تیار
رَبّا! ہُن کیہ کریے!
چارو طرف چلن دے چرچے
ہر سُو پئی پُکار
رَبّا! ہُن کیہ کریے!
ڈھانڈے کلیجے بل بل اُٹھدی
بِن دیکھے دیدار
رَبّا! ہُن کیہ کریے!
بُلھا! شوہ پیارے باجھوں
نہے اُرار نہ پار
رَبّا! ہُن کیہ کریے!

# Literal Translation

Friends are all falling like hair,
God, what shall I do?
        Nothing can induce them to stay;
        rather, they propagate despair.
                God, what shall I do?
        On all sides news of departures,
        and goodbyes echo in the air.
                God, what shall I do?
        Their memories tear me apart;
        I see them everywhere.
                God, what shall I do?
        Bulleh, your fondness is at fault;
        they are neither here, nor there.
                God, what shall I do?

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.53. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

The friends next door have risen and gone —
O Lord, what shall we do now!
They have risen and gone, they stay no longer;
the caravan is made ready.
O Lord, what shall we do now!
On all four sides the talk of leaving,
on every side a cry has risen.
O Lord, what shall we do now!
The heart, searching, blazes up in flame
without beholding the Beloved's sight.
O Lord, what shall we do now!
Bulleh! without the dear Lord
there is neither this shore nor the far.
O Lord, what shall we do now!
