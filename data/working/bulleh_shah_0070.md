# Original

پھاگن

پھاگن پھُلّے کھیت جیوں بَن ٹَن پھُول سنگار
ہر ڈالی پھُل پتّیاں؛ گل پھوُلن کے ہار

ہوری کھیلن سِتیاں پھاگن
میرے نَین جھلاریں وَگّن
اوکھے جیوَن دے دِن ٹگّن
سِینے بان پریم دے لگّن
ہوری ہو رہی

جو کُجھ روز اَزل تھِیں ہوئی
لِکھّی قلم نہ مِیٹے کوئی
دُکھاں سوُلاں دِتّی ڈھوئی
بُلّھا! شوہ نوں آکھو کوئی
جس نوں رو رہی

# Literal Translation

SPRING

In Spring the fields bloom; every branch is laden
with leaves and flowers like a garlanded woman.

          The girls are rapt in Holi
          while I weep solely
          who succumbed to the folly
          of an unrequited passion.

                    Holi! Holi!

          Nothing from that to this
          has changed. What was, is.
          That something is amiss
          love should be told.Mention

                    you miss him sorely.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.207. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

Spring

In Phalgun the fields blossom, decked out like a bride in flowers;
every branch bears leaf and blossom — garlands of blooms.

The girls play Holi in the spring,
while from my eyes the tears run in streams;
the days of my life pass hard and heavy,
the arrows of love strike deep in my breast —
and the Holi goes on being played.

Whatever was decreed from the day of eternity,
what the pen has written, none can erase.
Sorrows and torments have taken shelter in me.
Bulleh! let someone go and tell the Beloved,
the one for whom I weep.
