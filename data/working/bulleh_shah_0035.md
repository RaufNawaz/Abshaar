# Original

علموں بس کریں او یار

علموں بس کریں او یار!
اِکو الف تیرے درکار
علم نہ آوے وچ شُمار
جاندی عُمر، نہیں اعتبار
اِکو الف تیرے درکار
علموں بس کریں او یار!

پڑھ پڑھ، لِکھ لِکھ لاویں ڈھیر
ڈھیر کتاباں چار چوپھیر
گِردے جہالت، وچ اَنھیر
پُچھو: "راہ"؛ تے خبر نہ سار
علموں بس کریں او یار!

پڑھ پڑھ نفل نماز گُزاریں
اُچّیاں بانگاں چانگھاں ماریں
مِنبر تے چڑھ وعظ پُکاریں
کیتا تینوں علم خوار
علموں بس کریں او یار!

علموں پئے قضیّتے ہور
اَکھاں والے اَنھّے کور
پھِڑ دے سادھ، تے چھڈن چور
علموں بس کریں او یار!

پڑھ پڑھ شیخ مشائخ کہاویں
اُلٹے مسئلے گھڑوں بناویں
بے علماں نوں لُٹ لُٹ کھاویں
جھوٹے سچے کریں اقرار
علموں بس کریں او یار!

پڑھ پڑھ مُلاں ہوئے قاضی
اللہ علماں باجھوں راضی
ہووے حِرص دِنوں دِن تازی
تینوں کیتا حِرص خوار
علموں بس کریں او یار!

پڑھ پڑھ مسئلے پیا سُناویں
کھانا شک شُبھے دا کھاویں
دَسّیں ہور، تے ہور کماویں
اندر کھوٹ، باہر سُچیار
علموں بس کریں او یار!

جد میں سبق عشق دا پڑھیا
دریا دیکھ وحدت دا وڑیا
گھُمن گھیراں دے وچ اَڑیا
شاہ عنایت لایا پار!
علموں بس کریں او یار!

# Script Notes

- Script: Shahmukhi (Perso-Arabic)
- Language spans: Punjabi (with Arabic/Persian learned vocabulary — masla, qaazi, shaikh mashaaikh)
- Notes: Vision transcription of Rafat poem 34, read from the calligraphic Nastaliq facing pages (book pp.114 + 116 + 118). The LONGEST poem in the batch — it runs across THREE spreads. Refrain "Ilmon bas kareen o yaar! / Ikko Alif tere darkaar" (enough of learning — one Alif is all you need). Anti-scholastic/anti-clerical throughout; the final stanza names Bulleh's murshid Shah Inayat ("Shah Inayat laaya paar" — punning on inaayat = grace, which is why Rafat renders it "God's mercy"). Needs native/scholarly verification.

# Literal Translation

Enough of learning, friend.
One A can begin and end.

You study night and day
while life is ticking away,
when all you need is an A.
Enough of learning, friend,
    enough of learning!

The books you read and write
reach up to such a height
they've quite shut off the light.
You know not where you wend,
    enough of learning, friend.

The muezzin shatters the air
while you heap prayer on prayer
and sermons drip from your hair,
but to what end?
    Enough of learning, friend.

Has this knowledge brought relief
or added grief to grief?
You nab the just, and the thief
you fail to apprehend.
    Enough of learning, friend.

Such learning fools elevates
and some fresh bother creates,
is used to con illiterates,
make vows with false intent,
    enough of learning, friend.

Mullahs mug their way to power,
and now are judge and juror;
and avarice grows by the hour,
and straight to hell you wend.
    Enough of learning, friend.

You dream up problems in a wink,
schism is your meat and drink,
beneath saintly garb you stink,
saying what you don't intend,
    enough of learning, friend.

When the bait of Love I cast,
slippery Truth was hooked at last,
and the griefs which held me fast
God's mercy made an end.
    Enough of learning, friend!

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), pp.115+117+119. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training. Rafat renders "Ikko Alif tere darkaar" as "One A can begin and end" and turns the murshid's name (Shah Inayat) into "God's mercy."_

# AI Translation

Enough of learning, friend!
A single Alif is all you need.
Learning does not enter the reckoning;
your life slips away — there is no trusting it.
A single Alif is all you need.
Enough of learning, friend!

Reading and writing, you pile up heaps,
heaps of books all around you;
ignorance encircles you, darkness is within;
ask "the way" — and you have not the least news of it.
Enough of learning, friend!

You offer prayer upon voluntary prayer,
cry the call aloud, shout it high,
climb the pulpit and thunder sermons —
learning has only disgraced you.
Enough of learning, friend!

From learning, only more quarrels arise;
those with eyes are blind and sightless;
they seize the holy man and let the thief go free.
Enough of learning, friend!

Reading, reading, you style yourself Shaikh and elder,
you fabricate rulings upside-down,
you plunder the unlettered and devour them,
swearing the false and the true alike.
Enough of learning, friend!

Reading, reading, mullahs become qazis;
God is well pleased even without such learning.
Greed grows fresher by the day —
greed has disgraced you.
Enough of learning, friend!

Reading, reading, you keep reciting rulings,
you feed on the food of doubt and suspicion;
you say one thing and earn by another —
false within, upright without.
Enough of learning, friend!

When I read the lesson of love,
I beheld the river of oneness and plunged in;
I was caught fast in its whirlpools —
[my master] Shah Inayat carried me across!
Enough of learning, friend!
