# Original

بُلھے نالوں چُلھا چنگا

جِس پر تام پکائی دا

بُلھا شوہ نوں سوئی پاوے
جیہڑا بکرا بَنے قصائی دا

# Literal Translation

A stove is better than Bulleh,
it cooks something at least.

When beggars get together
of morsels they make a feast.

Love is a pinprick, Bulleh,
to a slaughterhouse beast.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.197. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._
