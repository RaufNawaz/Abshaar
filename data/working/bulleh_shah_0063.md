# Original

مَیں گل اوتھے دی کر داہاں!

نال رُوحاں دے لارا لایا
تُسی چلو! میں نالے آیا
ایتھے پردہ چپ بنایا
مَیں بھَرم بھلایا پھِر داہاں
مَیں گل اوتھے دی کر داہاں
پَر گل کر دا بھی ڈر داہاں

نال عالم دے کھیڈ اُساڈی
جے مَیں میری تاں وی پھاڈی
دھری دھرائی پُونجی ہاڈی
مَیں اگلا لیکھا بھر داہاں
مَیں گل اوتھے دی کر داہاں
پَر گل کر دا بھی ڈر داہاں

دے پُونجی مُورکھ جھنجھلایا
چوراں پِچھے پَیڑا لایا
چوراں دی مَیں پَیڑ لیایا
ہر شب دھاڑے دھر داہاں
مَیں گل اوتھے دی کر داہاں
پَر گل کر دا بھی ڈر داہاں

# Literal Translation

I told the spirits to go
and I would quickly follow,
but I threw up a screen, so
I would not stand out clear.

     I'm not talking of here,
     that's why I quake with fear.

This world has bound me fast;
even if I'm first, I'm last.
What use are these assets, alas,
my balance-sheet is up there.

     I'm not talking of here,
     that's why I quake with fear.

When robbed I shook with grief
and ran hard after the thief,
and found a clue. Now I've
made plundering my career.

     I'm not talking of here,
     that's why I quake with fear.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.193. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

I am speaking of that other place!

I made a promise with the spirits:
"You go ahead — I've come right behind you."
But here I hung a curtain of silence,
and, lost in illusion, still I wander.
I am speaking of that other place,
yet even to speak of it, I am afraid.

My game is played against the whole world;
if I say "mine," even that is torn from me.
The capital laid up and gathered — it is his;
I must yet settle the account that lies ahead.
I am speaking of that other place,
yet even to speak of it, I am afraid.

Handing over my capital, the fool grew flustered,
and set off in pursuit behind the thieves,
and I myself brought back the thieves' own track —
every night now I mount another raid.
I am speaking of that other place,
yet even to speak of it, I am afraid.
