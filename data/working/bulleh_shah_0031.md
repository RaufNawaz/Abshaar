# Original

روزے حج نماز نی مائے

روزے، حج، نماز نی مائے
مینوں پیا نے آن بھُلائے
جان پیا دیاں خبراں پئیاں
منطق، نحو سبھے بھُل گئیاں
اُس اَحد دے تار بجائے
روزے، حج، نماز نی مائے
مینوں پیا نے آن بھُلائے

جاں پیا میرے گھر آیا
بھُلّی مینوں شرح وقایہ
ہر مظہر وچ اوہا دِسدا
اندر باہر جلوہ اِس دا
بھُلّے لوکاں خبر نہ کائے
روزے، حج، نماز نی مائے
مینوں پیا نے آن بھُلائے

# Literal Translation

Fasting, pilgrimage, and the call
to prayer, love has drowned them all.
    Can any artifice survive
    when messages of love arrive?
    On the ears only its sounds fall.
        Fasting, pilgrimage, and the call
        to prayer, love has drowned them all.

The moment Love came toward
me, rituals went by the board.
Love, only love, was then to be seen,
over and under, around, between.
The loveless noticed nothing at all.
    Fasting, pilgrimage, and the call
    to prayer, love has drowned them all.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.105. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training. Rafat generalises "mantiq/nahv" to "artifice" and "Sharh al-Wiqaya" to "rituals"._

# AI Translation

Fasts, the Hajj, the prayer, O mother —
the Beloved came and made me forget them all.

When word of the Beloved reached me,
logic and grammar were all forgotten;
love struck the strings of that One alone.
Fasts, the Hajj, the prayer, O mother —
the Beloved came and made me forget them all.

The moment the Beloved entered my house,
I forgot even the Sharh al-Wiqaya;
in every manifestation He alone appears,
His radiance within and without.
The heedless folk have no inkling of it.
Fasts, the Hajj, the prayer, O mother —
the Beloved came and made me forget them all.
