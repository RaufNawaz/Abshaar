# Transliteration

vāre jāīe ohnāṉ toṉ, jehṛe māran gap-shap
gaudī labhī de dewan, te baghchā khāo ghap

# Literal Translation

Hats off to those who a lost coin reimburse,
but have no such scruple about a fat purse.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.191. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

We should be devoted to those, forsooth, who go about full of pious chatter:
who hand back a trifling cowrie they've found — yet gulp down a whole bundle [purse] in one gulp.
