# Original

الف اللہ دل رتّا میرا
مینوں ب دی خبر نہ کائی
ب پڑھیاں کجھ سمجھ نہ آوے
الف دی لذت آئی
ع تے غ دا فرق نہ جاناں
ایہ گل الف سمجھائی
بلھیا! قول الف دے پُرے
جہڑے دل دی کرن صفائی

# Script Notes

- Script: Shahmukhi (Perso-Arabic)
- Language spans: Punjabi
- Notes: Transcribed by visual reading of the calligraphic Nastaliq facing page (Taufiq Rafat, "A Selection", book p.34). Needs native/scholarly verification. The manuscript writes the bare letter ا to name the letter "Alif"; rendered here as الف for readability.

# Transliteration

Alif Allah, dil ratta mera
Mainu 'be' di khabar na kaai
Be parhiyan, kujh samajh na aawe
Alif di lazzat aai
'Ain te 'ghain da farq na jaanaan
Eh gal Alif samjhaai
Bulhiya! qaul Alif de pure
Jehre dil di karan safaai

# Literal Translation

A for Allah who has my heart,
I have no knowledge of B,
nor do I know what it means,
while A savours sweet to me.
I can't tell between O and Q,
it makes me dither and delve;
Bulleh, look after the first,
the rest will take care of itself.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.35. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training. Rafat maps the Urdu letters ain/ghain to English O/Q, so this is a literary adaptation._

# AI Translation

Alif — Allah — has dyed my heart;
of "be" I have no knowledge at all.
Reading "be", nothing comes clear to me,
while the sweetness of Alif has arrived.
I know no difference between ain and ghain —
this alone has Alif taught me.
Bulleh, hold fast the words of Alif
that scour the heart clean.
