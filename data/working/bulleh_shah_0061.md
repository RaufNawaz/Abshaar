# Original

کیوں عشق اُساں تے آیا اے

کیوں عشق اُساں تے آیا اے؟
تُوں آیا تے میں پایا اے

ابراہیم چا پخہ ستائیو
ذکریے سر کلو تر دھرائیو
یوسف ہٹو ہٹ وکائیو
کہو ساؤں کیہہ لیا یا اے

کیوں عشق اُساں تے آیا اے؟
تُوں آیا تے میں پایا اے

شیخ صنعان توں خوک چرائیو
شمس دی کھل الٹ لہائیو
سُولی تے منصور چڑھائیو
کر ہتھ ہُن کیس دل دھایا اے

کیوں عشق اُساں تے آیا اے؟
تُوں آیا تے میں پایا اے

جس گھر تیرا پھیرا هویا
سو جل بل کوئلہ ڈھیر هویا
جد راکھ اُڈی تد سیر هویا
کہو کس گل داس دھرایا اے

کیوں عشق اُساں تے آیا اے؟
تُوں آیا تے میں پایا اے

بُٹھا شوہ دے کارن کر
تن بھٹھی، مَن آہرن کر
وچ پریم ہتھوڑا مارن کر
لوہا بھنبیا، کیس اٹکایا اے

کیوں عشق اُساں تے آیا اے؟
تُوں آیا تے میں پایا اے

# Literal Translation

You came and I had to fall.
But why did you come at all?

    Abraham set alight,
    Zachariah sawn in half,
    Joseph was auctioned off.
    And what will be my lot?

        You came and I had to fall.
        But why did you come at all?

    Sanaan made to feed swine,
    Shams was skinned alive,
    Mansur crucified.
    Now I must toe the line.

You came and I had to fall.
But why did you come at all?

    Whatever house you tried
    was burnt to the ground;
    on the ash-heap you were found.
    Are you now satisfied?

        You came and I had to fall.
        But why did you come at all?

    Bulleh, do what you're told:
    turn your body to a stove
    for the forging of love.
    A red-hot rod now hold.

        You came and I had to fall.
        But why did you come at all?

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), pp.187 & 189. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

Why has love descended upon us?
You came, and I was caught [undone].

Abraham was set upon the pyre;
Zakariya's head was laid to the saw;
Yusuf was sold off, shop to shop —
say then, what has [this love] taken from us?

Why has love descended upon us?
You came, and I was caught.

Shaikh Sanʿan was set to herd swine;
Shams was flayed, his skin peeled off;
Mansur was raised upon the gallows —
so lay your hand [take heart]: on whom now has the heart been set?

Why has love descended upon us?
You came, and I was caught.

Whatever house you turned toward
burned down to a heap of coals;
and when the ash blew away, it was leveled —
say then, on what account has your servant been laid low?

Why has love descended upon us?
You came, and I was caught.

Sit down for the sake of the Beloved:
make your body the furnace, your heart the anvil;
strike the hammer of love within,
until the iron is beaten soft — on whom has [your heart] snagged?

Why has love descended upon us?
You came, and I was caught.
