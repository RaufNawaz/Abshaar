# Original

گنڈھاں

کیہو سُرتی گل کاج دی مَیں گنڈھاں کیتیاں پاواں
ساہے تے وِیج آوَسی، تُہن چاہلی گنڈھ گھتاواں
بابل آکھیا آن کے، تَیں ساہوریاں گھر جانا
رِیت اوتھوں دی اورَ ہَے، مُڑ پَیر نہ ایتھے پانا
گنڈھ پہلی نوں کھول کے، مَیں بَیٹھی بِرلاواں
اوڑک جاوَن جاوَتاں، تُہن مَیں داج رنگاواں
دیکھوں طرف بزار دی، سب رِستے لاگے
پلّے کُجھ نہ روکڑی، سب مُجھ سے بھاگے

کر بسم اللہ کھولیاں، مَیں گنڈھاں چاہلی
جس اپنا آپ ونجالیا، سو سُرجِن والی
وِیج سوہنی مَیں بھاوَندی، مُکلیندا آوَے
جس نوں عِشق ہَے لال دا، سو لال ہو جاوَے
عقل فِکر سب چھوڑ کے، شوہ نال سدھاوَے
گنڈھوں بِن، گل غیب دی اَساں یاد نہ کائے
تُہن اِنّا للہ آکھ کے، تُم کرو نِگاہیں
اللہ ہی سب ہو گیا، عبد اللہ نا ہیں

# Transliteration

gunḍhaan

keho surti gal kaaj di main gunḍhaan ketiaan paawaan
saahe te veej aavasi, tuhn chaahli gunḍh ghataawaan
baabal aakhiaa aan ke, tain saahoriaan ghar jaanaa
reet othon di aur hai, muṛ pair nah ethe paanaa
gunḍh pahli noon khol ke, main baiṭhi birlaawaan
auṛak jaavan jaavataan, tuhn main daaj rangaawaan
dekhoon taraf bazaar di, sab riste laage
palle kujh nah rokaṛi, sab mujh se bhaage

kar bismillaah kholiaan, main gunḍhaan chaahli
jis apnaa aap vanjaaliaa, so surjin vaali
veej sohni main bhaavandi, mukleendaa aave
jis noon ishq hai laal daa, so laal ho jaave
aqal fikar sab chhoṛ ke, shoh naal sidhaave
gunḍhon bin, gal ghaib di asaan yaad nah kaae
tuhn innaa lillaah aakh ke, tum karo nigaaheen
allaah hi sab ho gayaa, abdullaah naa hain

# Literal Translation

KNOTS

How many knots shall I tie,
                    dear, tell me true?
Forty days to the wedding,
                    so forty will do.
Father has told me I must
                    go to the bourne
of my in-laws, from which
                    none shall return.
The first knot I opened, and
                    grumbled and cried,
should the dresses be sequinned,
                    or should they be dyed?
I went off to the market with
                    an empty purse,
and the merchants supplied me
                    a push and a curse.

At last when the fortieth
                    knot was undone,
those who had finished their chores
                    was nary a one.
The loud procession is here,
                    this is the day
when the ready and the unready will
                    be taken away,
to be with the master and
                    sit by his side
remembering nothing at all but
                    she's his bride.
Now the last rites are ended, and
                    the bright room
is empty of guests. No one there
                    but bride and groom.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), pp.211, 213. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

Knots

What sense have I of the wedding-business — how many knots am I to tie?
The date will come at the fixed hour; let me quickly tie the knots.
Father said, coming to me: you must go to your in-laws' house;
the custom there is otherwise — once gone, you shall never set foot here again.
Undoing the first knot, I sat and wept aloud.
The days of leaving run out at last; now let me have my dowry dyed.
I look toward the marketplace — every relation has set off,
nothing in my pocket, no ready cash; they have all fled from me.

Saying "Bismillah," I began to undo the knots quickly.
Whoever has lost her own self, she is the one who reaches the Beloved.
Lovely is the date; longing, I wait — the one who takes the bride away is coming.
Whoever loves the Beloved becomes the Beloved.
Leaving behind all reason and worry, she sets out with the Bridegroom.
Without untying the knots, we gave no thought to the matter of the Unseen.
Now say "Inna lillah" and cast your gaze:
All has become God alone — there is no servant of God any more.
