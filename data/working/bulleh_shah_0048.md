# Original

میری بُکّل دے وچ چور!

میری بُکّل دے وچ چوری، میری بُکّل دے وچ چور

ساڈھو کِس نوں کوک سُناواں: میری بُکّل دے وچ چور
چوری چوری نِکل گیا تے جگ وچ پے گیا شور
میری بُکّل دے وچ چور

جِس جاتا تِس جان لیا، اتے ہور ہوئے جھمّر مور
تھَک گئے سارے جھگڑے جھیڑے، نِکل پیا کوئی ہور
میری بُکّل دے وچ چور

عرش منتور بانگاں مِلیاں، سُنیاں تخت لہور
شاہ عنائت گنڈیاں پائیاں، تھَک چُپ رَکھدا دُور
میری بُکّل دے وچ چوری، میری بُکّل دے وچ چور

jis jātā tis jān liyā, ate hor hoe jhammar mor

arsh mantūr bāṅgāṅ miliyāṅ, suṇiyāṅ takht lahaur
shāh ináyat gaṇḍiyāṅ pāiyāṅ, thak chup rakhdā dūr
merī bukkal de vich chorī, merī bukkal de vich chor

# Literal Translation

I'm harbouring a thief.
Since he wears my guise how can I give him away?
If he tiptoes out there will be hell to pay.
        I'm harbouring a thief.

Some will understand, others will scratch their head.
Will the pother end if he's not the one they expect?
        I'm harbouring a thief.

The rulers of Lahore under threatening skies
put their hands to their ears and shut their eyes.
        I'm harbouring a thief.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.155. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

There is a thief tucked in the fold of my shawl!

Within the fold of my shawl is a theft; within my shawl there is a thief.

Friends, to whom shall I cry out and tell: there is a thief in the fold of my shawl.
Stealthily he slipped away, and an uproar broke out in the world.
        A thief in the fold of my shawl.

Whoever recognized him understood the secret, while the others kept up their whirling dance and clamour.
All the quarrels and squabbles wore themselves out — some other one had slipped away.
        A thief in the fold of my shawl.

From the heavens the call to prayer was answered, the throne of Lahore heard it.
Shah Inayat has tied the knots; grown weary, he keeps his silence far off.
        Within the fold of my shawl is a theft; within my shawl there is a thief.
