# Original

مُنہ آئی بات نہ رہندی اے

سچ کہواں تے بھانبڑ مچدا اے
جھوٹھ آکھیاں کُجھ نہ بچدا اے
جی دوہاں گلاں توں جچدا اے
سچ سچ کے جیہا کہندی اے
مُنہ آئی بات نہ رہندی اے

ایہہ تلکن بازی وِہڑا اے
تم تم کے تُرو اندھیرا اے
وَر اندر دیکھو کیہڑا اے
کیوں خلقت باہر ڈھونڈ دی نیندی اے
مُنہ آئی بات نہ رہندی اے

جِس پایا بھیت قلندر دا
راہ کھوجیا اپنے اندر دا
اوہ واسی ہے سُکھ مندر دا
جِتّھے کوئی نہ چڑھدی لہندی اے
مُنہ آئی بات نہ رہندی اے

اِک لازم شرطِ اُدب دی اے
سانوں بات معلومی سب دی اے
ہر ہر وِچ صُورت رب دی اے
کِتّے ظاہر، کِتّے چھپیندی اے
مُنہ آئی بات نہ رہندی اے

اَساں پڑھیا عِلم تحقیقی اے
اوتھے اِکو حَرف حقیقی اے
ہور جھگڑا سب ودھیکی اے
ایویں رَولا پا پا بہندی اے
مُنہ آئی بات نہ رہندی اے

شوہ بُلھا! ساں تِیں دُکھ نہیں
بِن شوہ دے دُوجا سُکھ نہیں
پر دیکھن والی اَکھ نہیں
تہِیں جان جُدائیاں سہندی اے
مُنہ آئی بات نہ رہندی اے

munh āī bāt nah rahndī ae

sach kahvāṅ te bhāṅbaṛ machdā ae

jī dohāṅ galāṅ tūṅ jachdā ae

munh āī bāt nah rahndī ae

eh talkan bāzī vihṛā ae
tam tam ke turo andherā ae

kyūṅ khalqat bāhar ḍhūṇḍdī nīndī ae

jis pāyā bhet qalandar dā
rāh khojiyā apne andar dā

jitthe koī nah chaṛhdī lahndī ae
munh āī bāt nah rahndī ae

ik lāzim shart-i udab dī ae
sānūṅ bāt maalūmī sab dī ae

kitte zāhir, kitte chhapendī ae
munh āī bāt nah rahndī ae

othe iko haraf haqīqī ae
hor jhagṛā sab vadhīkī ae
eveṅ raulā pā pā bahndī ae
munh āī bāt nah rahndī ae

shoh bullhā! sāṅ tīṅ dukh nahīṅ
bin shoh de dūjā sukh nahīṅ
par dekhan vālī akh nahīṅ
tahīṅ jān judāiyāṅ sahndī ae
munh āī bāt nah rahndī ae

# Literal Translation

Truth will out

        though it adds fuel to fire.
        Lies are a burnt-out pyre.
        So after looking at both
                the tongue must shout,
                truth will out.

        In the darkness walk with care
        for floors are slippery here.
        Ascertain who is within
                while others hunt without.
                Truth will out.

        Who knows what the sadhu knows
        will surely find repose.
        On the tideless river of peace
                he is afloat.
                Truth will out.

        Whatever the time and place
        God is in every face.
        Unalterable this fact
                covert or not.
                Truth will out.

        The lessons experience taught
        have all been dearly bought.
        The rest is a stream's babble
                not worth a thought.
                Truth will out.

        Love, Bulleh, is part of us
        without which is nothing but fuss.
        If only we had the eyes
                we would not doubt.
                Truth will out.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.159–161. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

The word that rises to the lips will not stay unspoken

If I speak the truth, a blaze flares up;
if I tell a lie, nothing is saved.
The heart weighs both these matters —
truth speaks out just as truth is.
The word that rises to the lips will not stay unspoken.

This is a slippery playing-field, a courtyard;
tread step by step, for it is dark.
Look which one is the bridegroom within —
why does creation go searching outside?
The word that rises to the lips will not stay unspoken.

Whoever has grasped the secret of the qalandar,
who has sought the path within himself,
he is a dweller in the temple of bliss,
a place where none rises and none sets.
The word that rises to the lips will not stay unspoken.

One condition is binding — the courtesy of restraint;
to us the matter of all is known.
In each and every one is the face of the Lord,
somewhere manifest, somewhere hidden.
The word that rises to the lips will not stay unspoken.

We have studied the science of inquiry —
there is but one true letter there;
all the rest of the wrangling is excess,
people just sit raising a needless din.
The word that rises to the lips will not stay unspoken.

O my Beloved, Bulleh! Away from you there is no grief that is grief;
without the Beloved there is no other joy.
But there is no eye that truly sees,
and so the soul endures its separations.
The word that rises to the lips will not stay unspoken.
