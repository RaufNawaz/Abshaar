# Original

مَینوں چھڈ گئے

مَینوں چھڈ گئے، آپ لد گئے
مَیں وِچ کیہہ تقصیر

راتیں نیندر نہ سُکھ سُتّی
دِنے پلٹیا زیر
مَیں وِچ کیہہ تقصیر

چھُریاں تے تلواراں کولوں
تِکھّے عشق دے تیر
مَیں وِچ کیہہ تقصیر

عشقے جیہڈ نہ ظالم کوئی
ایہہ زحمت بے پیر
مَیں وِچ کیہہ تقصیر؟

# Literal Translation

He has left me to my fears.

     What was my sin?

Sleep is a stranger now.
I'm streaked with tears.

     What was my sin?

Arrows of love are keener
than knives and spears.

     What was my sin?

For sheer cruelty, love
has no peers.

     What was my sin?

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.195. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

They have deserted me and gone away.

They abandoned me, they themselves departed —
what fault of mine was there in this?

By night no sleep, no easy rest,
by day I have been ground beneath.
What fault of mine was there in this?

Sharper than the daggers and the swords
are the piercing arrows of love.
What fault of mine was there in this?

There is no tyrant to equal love,
this affliction without master or guide.
What fault of mine was there in this?
