# Original

دھرمسال دھاڑوی وسدے

دھرمسال دھاڑوی وسدے، ٹھاکر دوارے ٹھگ
وچ مسیت کوسے تے رہندے، عاشق رہن الگ

# Transliteration

Dharamsaal dhaaṛvi vasde, ṭhaakur duaare ṭhag
Vich maseet kose te rehnde, aashiq rahn alag

# Literal Translation

Merchants in mosque and monastery, thugs in the landlord's pay.
The lover alone goes his secret, inviolable way.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.103. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

Robbers lodge in the temple rest-house, cheats at the idol's shrine-gate;
