# Original

کُتّے تیتھوں اُتّے

راتیں جاگیں، کریں عبادت
راتیں جاگن کُتّے، تیتھوں اُتّے

بھونکن توں بند مُول نہ ہندے
جا رُوڑی تے سُتّے: تیتھوں اُتّے

خصم اپنے دا دَر نہ چھڈ دے
بھانویں وَجن جُتّے: تیتھوں اُتّے

بُلھے شاہ! کوئی رخت وِہاج لَے
نہیں تے بازی لَے گئے کُتّے، تیتھوں اُتّے

# Transliteration

Kutte teethon utte

Raateen jaageen, karen ibaadat
Raateen jaagan kutte, teethon utte

Bhaunkan toon band mool na hunde
Jaa rooṛi te sutte: teethon utte

Khasam apne da dar na chhaḍ de
Bhaanven vajan jutte: teethon utte

Bulhe Shah! koi rakht vihaaj lai
Naheen te baazi lai gaye kutte, teethon utte

# Literal Translation

You stay up all night to pray,
but dogs are sleepless too;
    they're better than you.

To bark and bark, and then to sleep
on the first trash-heap you view;
    they're better than you.

They will not leave their master's house
though beaten black and blue;
    they're better than you.

Bulleh, you'd better mend your ways,
else it will be proved true:
    dogs are better than you.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.125. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

Dogs are above you.

You keep vigil at night, performing your worship;
dogs keep awake at night too — they are above you.

They never once leave off their barking,
then go and sleep on the rubbish-heap: above you.

They will not abandon their master's door,
though they be beaten with shoes: above you.

Bulleh Shah! trade for some worthy merchandise,
or else the dogs have won the game — they are above you.
