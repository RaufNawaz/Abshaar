# Original

"رانجھا رانجھا" کر دی

"رانجھا رانجھا" کر دی ہُن میں آپے رانجھا ہوئی
سَدو مینوں دِھیدو رانجھا، "ہیر" نہ آکھو کوئی

رانجھا میں وچ، میں رانجھے وچ، غیر خیال نہ کوئی
میں نہیں، اوہ آپے ہے، اپنی آپ کرے دِلجوئی

جو کجھ ساڈے اندر وَستے، ذات اساڈی سوئی
جس دے نال میں نیونھ لگایا، اوہو جیسی ہوئی

چِٹّی چادر لاہ سُٹ کُڑیے، پہن فقیراں لوئی
چِٹّی چادر داغ لگیسی، لوئی داغ نہ کوئی

تخت ہزارے لَے چل بُلھیا، سیالیں مِلے نہ ڈھوئی
"رانجھا رانجھا" کر دی ہُن میں آپے رانجھا ہوئی

# Transliteration

"Ranjha Ranjha" kar di

"Ranjha Ranjha" kar di, hun main aape Ranjha hoi
Sado mainu Dhido Ranjha, "Heer" na aakho koi

Ranjha main vich, main Ranjhe vich, ghair khayaal na koi
Main naheen, oh aape hai, apni aap kare diljoi

Jo kujh saaḍe andar vaste, zaat asaaḍi soi
Jis de naal main neonh lagaaya, oho jaisi hoi

Chiṭṭi chaadar laah suṭ kuṛiye, pahin faqeeraan loi
Chiṭṭi chaadar daagh lagesi, loi daagh na koi

Takht Hazaare lai chal Bulhiya, Siyaalein mile na ḍhoi
"Ranjha Ranjha" kar di, hun main aape Ranjha hoi

# Literal Translation

'Ranjha, Ranjha' I cried till only Ranjha is there?
I'm transformed, now Heer has disappeared.

We are synonymous: one heart, a single thought,
I am expunged, and Ranjha stands out clear.

Since he is inside me, he is all that exists,
that which I show to the world is just veneer.

Don the sackcloth, throw the white dress away,
for they are covered with stains who whiteness wear.

Hazara calls me, in Sial no friends are near.
'Ranjha, Ranjha' I cried till only Ranjha is here.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.101. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training. (Scan contains typos: "Ranhja", "trarnsformed".)_

# AI Translation

Crying out "Ranjha, Ranjha," I have myself now become Ranjha.
Call me Dhido-Ranjha now; let no one call me "Heer."

Ranjha is within me, and I within Ranjha — no other thought remains;
I am not: it is He alone, consoling His own self.

Whatever dwells within us, that is our very essence;
the one I gave my love to — I have become just like him.

Cast off the white sheet, girl, and put on the faqir's coarse blanket;
the white sheet will show every stain, but the blanket takes no stain.

Take me to Takht Hazara, Bulleh — among the Sials I find no refuge.
Crying out "Ranjha, Ranjha," I have myself now become Ranjha.
