# Original

مَیں گسنبا چُن چُن ہاری

مَیں کسنبا چُن چُن ہاری

ایس گسنب دے خار بھلیرے! اُڑ اُڑ چُنتی پاڑی
ایس گسنب دا حاکم کڑاء، ظالم ہَے پٹواری
ایس گسنب دے چار مُقدم، ملبہ منگدے بھاری
ہورناں چگیا پھویا پھویا، بھر لئی مَیں پٹیاری
چِگ چِگ کے جو ڈھیری کیتا، اَن لَیندے بیوپاری

اوکھی گھاٹی مُشکل پَینڈا، سِرتے گٹھڑی بھاری
عملاں والیاں سَب لنگھ گئیاں، رہ گئی اوگنہاری
ساری عُمرا کھیڈ گوائی اور تک بازی ہاری
مَیں کمین، اُچّی، کوہجی، بے گُن کون وِچاری
بُلّھا شوہ دے لائق ناہیں، شاہ عنایت تاری
مَیں کسنبا چُن چُن ہاری

# Literal Translation

What I've picked up costs me dear.

Its thorns are sharp, my clothes are torn,
I must deal with one cold to the bone,
his servants tax each stick I own
which is quite a bundle.  As soon
as it is tied, the merchants will be here.

What I've picked up costs me dear.

The slope is steep, and heavy the load
on my head, and I stumble on the road.
My misspent life tells on my face,
I am so weak and lacking in grace,
not Bulleh, but Inayat must steer.

What I've picked up costs me dear.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.169. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

I have picked the safflowers one by one — and lost.

The thorns of this safflower-bush are cruel: flying about, my veil is torn.
The overseer of this bush is harsh, and the record-keeper is a tyrant.
This bush has four headmen who demand a heavy levy.
For others I gathered blossom by blossom, and filled up my basket-cloth.
Whatever I picked and heaped up, the merchants carry off.

The pass is hard, the road is difficult, and the bundle on my head is heavy.
The virtuous ones have all crossed over; only I, the sinner, am left behind.
I have thrown away my whole life at play, and lost the game at the last throw.
I am low-born, loud, ill-favoured, without merit — a wretched thing.
Bulleh is not worthy of the Beloved; Shah Inayat, ferry me across.
I have picked the safflowers one by one — and lost.
