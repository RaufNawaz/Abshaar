# Original

اپنے سنگ رلائیں پیارے! اپنے سنگ رلائیں
راہ پُواں تے دھاڑے بیلی؛ جنگل، رکھ، بلائیں
بگھمن پیتے، چت مچتے، بگھنے روکن راہیں
تیرے پار جگا دھر چڑھیا، کنڈے لکھ بلائیں
ہول دِلے توں تھر تھر کنبدا، بیڑا پار لنگھائیں
بلھے شاہ نوں شوہ دا مکھڑا، گھونگھٹ کھول دکھائیں
اپنے سنگ رلائیں پیارے! اپنے سنگ رلائیں

# Transliteration

Apne sang rulaaeen pyaare! apne sang rulaaeen
Raah puaan te dhaare beli; jangal, rakh, balaaeen
Baghman piite, chit machte, baghne rokan raaheen
Tere paar jaga dhar charhya, kande lakh balaaeen
Haul dile tun thar thar kanbda, bera paar langhaaeen
Bulhe Shah noon shoh da mukhra, ghoonghat khol dikhaaeen
Apne sang rulaaeen pyaare! apne sang rulaaeen

# Literal Translation

Embrace me, love, and hold me next your heart.
What sinister jungles stand in my way,
what snakes, what beasts of prey,
and on the other side what spirits start.
Reach out your hand to me so fear may cease.
One glimpse of you and I shall be at peace.

Embrace me, love, and hold me next your heart.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.51. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

Merge me into Yourself, beloved! Merge me into Yourself.
The roads are beset with raiders, comrade — jungle, thicket, terrors;
[wild beasts lie in wait,] they block the paths;
on the far side of You the ground rears up, with a hundred thousand thorns and dangers.
With dread the heart trembles and shakes — ferry my boat across.
Show Bulleh Shah the Beloved's face; lift the veil and reveal it.
Merge me into Yourself, beloved! Merge me into Yourself.
