# Original

اِک نقطے وچ گل مُکدی اے

پھڑ نقطہ، چھوڑ حِساباں نوں
چھڈ دوزخ، گور عذاباں نوں
کر بند کفر دیاں باباں نوں
کر صاف دِلاں دیاں خاباں نوں
گل ایسے گھر وچ ڈُھکدی اے
اِک نقطے وچ گل مُکدی اے

ایویں مَتّھا زمیں گھسائیدا
پا لمّا محراب دِکھائیدا
پڑھ کلمہ لوک ہسائیدا
دِل اندر سمجھ نہ لائیدا
گدی سچی بات وی مُکدی اے
اِک نقطے وچ گل مُکدی اے

اِک جنگل بھجریں جاندے نیں
اِک دانہ روز دا کھاندے نیں
بے سمجھ وجود تھکاندے نیں
گھر آوَن ہو کے ماندے نیں
پچھلیاں اندر جِند شکدی اے
اِک نقطے وچ گل مُکدی اے

کئی حاجی بن بن آئے جی
گل نیلے جامے پائے جی
حج وچ پیچ، ٹھمکے لے کھائے جی
پر ایہہ گل کیہنوں بھائے جی
کِتّے سچی گل وی رُکدی اے
اِک نقطے وچ گل مُکدی اے

# Literal Translation

One is enough.  Break the counting-frames,
forget hell's terrors and its flames,
purify your dreams and desires,
belief and unbelief are just names.

    Truth stands in the hall.
    One point settles it all.

In prayer why abrade your forehead?
Away with it, tear down the facade
of morality.  Causing people pain
is the only sin you should dread.

    The writing is on the wall.
    One point settles it all.

To the same jungle the hawks repair
and seed by seed they scrounge there;
in this their energy is spent,
and they are left gasping for air.

    Prayer is body's gall.
    One point settles it all.

From a pilgrimage some have returned,
but any merit they may have earned,
despite humility in dress,
in percentage and shady deals is burned.

    Truth stands too tall.
    One point settles it all.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), pp.61+63. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training. Rafat renders the refrain freely (each stanza's first refrain-line varies while "One point settles it all" recurs) and adapts the imagery._

# AI Translation

In one point the whole matter is closed.

Seize the point, let go of your reckonings;
give up dread of hell and the grave's torments.
Bolt shut the doors of unbelief,
scour clean the dreamings of the heart —
the matter comes home to such a house:
in one point the whole matter is closed.

For nothing you grind your brow on the ground,
prostrate long to show off at the prayer-niche,
recite the creed to make people gape,
yet plant no understanding inside the heart.
Even the true word one day runs out:
in one point the whole matter is closed.

Off to a forest the [birds] fly,
living on a single grain a day;
the mindless only wear their bodies out
and drag themselves home spent.
[The soul frets deep within]:
in one point the whole matter is closed.

Many come back turned into pilgrims,
draping blue robes about themselves,
strutting and swaying through the Hajj —
but whom does such a show please?
Somewhere even the true word is stopped:
in one point the whole matter is closed.
