# Original

ہُن میں لکھیا سوہنا یار

جدوں اَحد اِک اِکلا سی، نہ ظاہر کوئی تجلا سی
نہ رب رسول نہ اللہ سی، نہ سی جبار تے نہ قہار

بے چُون و بے چگُون سی، بے شبہے تے بے نمونہ سی
نہ کوئی رنگ نمونہ سی، ہُن ہویا گوناگُون ہزار

پھیر کُن کیہا فیکون کمایا، بے چُونی توں چُون بنایا
احد دے وچ میم رلایا، تاہیوں کیتا احمد پیار

ہُن میں لکھیا سوہنا یار، جس دے حُسن دا گرم بازار

پیر پیغمبر اُس دے بردے، اِنس ملائک سجدے کردے
سر قدماں دے اُتے دھردے، سب توں وڈی اوہ سرکار

تجوں مسیت تجوں بُت خانہ، برتی رہاں نہ روزہ جاناں
بھُلا وضو، نماز دوگانہ، تینیں پر جان کراں نثار

جو کوئی اُس نوں لکھیا چاہے، بے وسیلے نہ لکھیا جائے
شاہ عنایت بھیت بتائے، تاہیں کھُلے سب اسرار

ہُن میں لکھیا سوہنا یار، جس دے حُسن دا گرم بازار

# Literal Translation

I've tracked the beauty down
which was the talk of the town.

    A Word existed in space
    which had no form or face.
    None mighty or small.
    No God or prophet at all.

I've tracked the beauty down
which was the talk of the town.

    The Word had no sire or heir,
    it was beyond compare.
    It had no colour or smell.
    There was no heaven or hell.

I've tracked the beauty down
which was the talk of the town.

    Then the Word was mated
    and angels and saints created
    to choir around His seat,
    and kneel at His feet.

I've tracked the beauty down
which was the talk of the town.

    And God was temple and mosque
    and all you could ask,
    but cold to fast and prayer-call.
    He was above them all.

I've tracked the beauty down
which was the talk of the town.

    Only the proper guide
    can lead you to His side;
    and unless you choose
    Inayat, you will lose.

I've tracked the beauty down
which was the talk of the town.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), pp.183, 185. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

Now I have written down the beautiful Beloved, whose beauty holds a hot market [is in great demand].

When the One (aḥad) was alone, single — no manifest radiance shone forth,
there was no Lord, no Messenger, no Allah; neither the Almighty nor the Wrathful was yet.

Without quality and without likeness He was, without doubt and without pattern;
no colour or model was there — now He has become a thousandfold, of every hue.

Then He spoke "Be!" and made "it becomes" (kun fayakūn), out of the quality-less He fashioned quality;
into the "one" (aḥad) He mingled a "mīm" — and so He loved [it as] "Aḥmad."

Now I have written down the beautiful Beloved, whose beauty holds a hot market.

Saints and prophets are His slaves; men and angels bow in prostration,
they lay their heads upon His feet — He is the highest court of all.

For Him I would forsake the mosque, forsake the idol-temple; I keep no fast, know no ritual observance,
forget ablution and the two-cycle prayer — upon You I would offer up my very life.

Whoever would seek to write [know] Him, cannot write [reach] Him without a means [a guide];
let Shāh ʿInāyat reveal the secret, and then all mysteries are opened.

Now I have written down the beautiful Beloved, whose beauty holds a hot market.
