# Original

جیسی صورت ع دی، ویسی صورت غ
اک نقطے دا فرق ہے: بھلی پھرے گوئیں

# Script Notes

- Script: Shahmukhi (Perso-Arabic)
- Language spans: Punjabi
- Notes: Vision transcription of the calligraphic Nastaliq facing page (Taufiq Rafat "A Selection", Rafat poem 2, book p.36). Needs native verification. A couplet on the letters ain (ع) and ghain (غ), which differ by a single dot (nuqta).

# Transliteration

Jaisi soorat 'ain di, waisi soorat ghain
Ik nuqte da farq hai: bhalli phire ku'in

# Literal Translation

O and Q are much alike, except for a squiggle;
to think one tiny stroke has made the whole world wriggle.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.37. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training. Rafat maps the Urdu letters ain/ghain to English O/Q._

# AI Translation

The form of 'ain is just like ghain;
a single dot sets them apart — yet over that one dot the bewildered world keeps wandering.
