# Original

مَیں اُڈیکاں کر رہی

مَیں اُڈیکاں کر رہی، گیدی آ، کر پھیرا
پچھماں سیج وِچھائیاں، دِل کیتا وہڑا
توں لُک کیندا آوڑیں شاہ عنائت میرا
مَیں اُڈیکاں کر رہی، گیدی آ، کر پھیرا

اوہ اجیہا کون ہَے جا آکھے جیہڑا
مَیں وِچ کیہ تقصیر ہَے، مَیں بردا تیرا
تَیں باجھوں مرا کون ہَے، دِل ڈھانہ میرا
مَیں اُڈیکاں کر رہی، گیدی آ، کر پھیرا

ہتھ کنگن، بانھ چُوڑیاں، گل نو رنگ چولا
ماہی مَینوں کر گیا کوئی رَاول رَولا
جُھل بُھل ڈھائیں ماریاں، دِل پتّھر تیرا
مَیں اُڈیکاں کر رہی، گیدی آ، کر پھیرا

بُلھیا! شوہ دے واسطے دِل بھڑکن بھائیں
اوکھا پَینڈا پریم دا، دُکھ گھٹدا نا ہیں
دِل وِچ دھڑکے جھیڑ دے، سِر دھائیں میرا
مَیں اُڈیکاں کر رہی، گیدی آ، کر پھیرا

# Literal Translation

I'm waiting, pass this way once more,
Inayat Shah, my eyes are sore
with looking for you at the door.

        I'm waiting, pass this way once more.

Who will lead me to the place
of my master, so by his grace
he may my heart and body restore.

        I'm waiting, pass this way once more.

See, I am dressed to the nines
for that cold man.  I see no signs
of him, and am stricken to the core.

        I'm waiting, pass this way once more.

Bulleh, every beat of your heart
cries love, but the tears start
to the eyes when they see what's in store.

        I'm waiting, pass this way once more.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.165. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

I keep on waiting

I keep on waiting — come round, pass this way once more.
I have spread the bed at dusk, my heart has grown desolate.
Where have you hidden yourself — come, my Shah Inayat.
I keep on waiting — come round, pass this way once more.

Who is there of a kind to go and speak my word,
what fault is in me, I who am your slave?
Apart from you who is mine — my heart is breaking.
I keep on waiting — come round, pass this way once more.

Bangles on my wrist, glass bracelets on my arm, a bright-coloured tunic at my throat;
some beloved has raised a great clamour in me.
Swaying, I sink down again and again — your heart is stone.
I keep on waiting — come round, pass this way once more.

O Bulleh! for the Beloved's sake the heart blazes and burns;
hard is the road of love, the pain never lessens.
The quarrel throbs within my heart — I bow my head.
I keep on waiting — come round, pass this way once more.
