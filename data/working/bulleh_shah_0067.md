# Original

تُسی کرو اساڈی کاری

تُسی کرو اَساڈی کاری
نی کوئی ہو گئی دین بھاری

ایہہ دِل میرے وِچ وَسدا
بہہ نال اَساڈے ہَسدا
پُچھّی آں باتاں تاں اُٹھ اُڈدا
لے کے بازاں وانگ اُڈاری
تُسی کرو اَساڈی کاری

ہُن مَیں شوہ دریاواں پایاں
ٹھاٹھاں لہراں دے مُونہہ آیاں
گُھمَن گھیراں پکڑ بھوایاں
اوپروں برکھا رین اندھاری
تُسی کرو اَساڈی کاری

وے تَیں کیہے پینگھر پائے
تارے کھاری بہیٹھھ لُکائے
مونجھ دی رَسّی ناگ بنائے
تیرے سہراں توں بلہاری
تُسی کرو اَساڈی کاری

# Literal Translation

Take me, love, under your care;
my woes are too much to bear.

     This troubled heart lacks grace
     and laughs in my face.
     When I ask a question
     it takes like a hawk to the air.

          Take me, love, under your care;
          my woes are too much to bear.

     Love is a raging river
     in whose tumult I quiver
     and which sucks me under,
     while dark clouds shake their hair.

          Take me love, under your care:
          my woes are too much to bear.

     In the basket you tote
     pains writhe. A coiled rope
     gleams like a snake.
     None can escape your snare.

          Take me, love, under your care;
          my woes are too much to bear.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.201. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

You take charge of my affair.

Take charge of my affair —
some heavy debt has fallen due.

This heart that dwells within me
sits beside me and laughs at me;
when I ask it questions, it starts up and flies off,
soaring away like a hawk.
You take charge of my affair.

Now I have plunged into the deep rivers,
come face to face with the surging waves;
the whirlpools have seized and spun me round,
and overhead the rain, the dark of night.
You take charge of my affair.

What swings have you set me on?
You hid the stars in a basket;
you turned the rope of rushes into a serpent.
I am a sacrifice to your wedding-crowns.
You take charge of my affair.
