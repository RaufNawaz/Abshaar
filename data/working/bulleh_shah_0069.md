# Original

جُمعرات

جُمعرات سُہاوَنی دُکھ درد نہ آہا پاپ
اوہ جامہ ساڈا پہن کے آئے تماشے آپ

اگّوں آ گئی جُمعرات
شرابوں لاگا گِر رِلی برات
لاگا، مَست، پیالہ ہات
مَینوں بُھلّی ذات صفات
دیوانی ہو رہی

اَیسی زَحمت لوک نہ پاوَن
مُلّاں گھمول تعویذ پِلاوَن
پڑھ من عزیمت، جِن بُھلاوَن
سِتیاں شاہ مدار کھڈاوَن
مَیں چُپ ہو رہی

# Literal Translation

THURSDAY

This is a Thursday. Pain is no sin.
Wearing my dress, love has wandered in.
          A good day to be up
          with wine enough to drown in,
          as I stagger with a cup
          all else forgotten.

                    O I'm in ecstasy!

          Think of the simpletons
          who go to be exorcised
          by devilish priests of demons,
          when all they need is a guide

                    to set them free.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.205. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

Thursday

Thursday is lovely — no grief, no pain, no sin was there:
He Himself has come, putting on our garb, to make a spectacle.

Thursday has come round again.
A wedding-throng has gathered, drunk with wine;
tipsy, intoxicated, cup in hand —
I have forgotten essence and attributes,
and become one possessed.

May no one bear such affliction:
the mullahs grind up amulets and make one drink them,
they chant spells of exorcism to drive out the spirits;
the demon Shah Madar makes the sleeping ones dance —
but I have fallen silent.
