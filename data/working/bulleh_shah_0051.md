# Original

مَولا آدمی بَن آیا

مَولا آدمی بَن آیا

آپے آہو، آپے چیتا، آپے مارن دھایا
آپے صاحِب، آپے بردا، آپے مُل وِکایا
مَولا آدمی بَن آیا

بازیگر کیہ بازی کھیڈی، مَینوں پُتلی وانگ نچایا
مَیں اُس تالی پر نچناں ہاں جو گِت مت یار لکھایا
مَولا آدمی بَن آیا

# Literal Translation

God has come down as man.

        He is deer and leopard, kills and is killed,
        he is master and slave, sells and is sold.

God has come down as man.

        I am a puppet on a string, and such
        is his control, I tremble at a touch.

God has come down as man.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.163. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

The Lord has become man.

The Lord has become man.

He himself is the deer, himself the leopard, himself the one who springs to kill;
himself the master, himself the slave, himself sold at a price.
The Lord has become man.

What a game the juggler has played — he has made me dance like a puppet.
I dance to whatever measure the Friend has taught and written for me.
The Lord has become man.
