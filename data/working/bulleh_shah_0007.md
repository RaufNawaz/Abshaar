# Original

اپنا دَس ٹِکانا؛ کدھروں آیا، کدھر جانا؟
جس ٹھانے دا مان کریں توں
اوہنے تیرے نال نہ جانا
ظلم کریں تے لوک ستاویں
کِسب پھڑیو لُٹ کھانا
کرکے چاؤڑ چار دِہاڑے
اوڑک توں اُٹھ جانا
شہر خموشاں دے چل وسیئے
جِتھے مُلک سمانا
بھر بھر پُور لنگھاوے، ڈاہڈا
مَلک الموت مُہانا
ایہناں سبھناں تھیں ہے بلّھا
او گنہگار پُرانا
اپنا دَس ٹِکانا؛ کدھروں آیا، کدھر جانا؟

# Transliteration

Apna das tikaana; kidhron aaya, kidhar jaana?
Jis thaane da maan karein tun
Ohne tere naal na jaana
Zulm karein te lok sataavein
Kisb pharyo lut khaana
Karke chaaor chaar dihaare
Orak tun uth jaana
Shahr khamoshaan de chal vaseeye
Jithe mulk samaana
Bhar bhar poor langhaave, daahda
Malak-ul-maut muhaana
Eihnaan sabhnaan theen hai Bulleh
O gunahgaar puraana
Apna das tikaana; kidhron aaya, kidhar jaana?

# Literal Translation

Where is your home?
Whence did you come?
Where do you go?

The place you pride in
will go for a song;
kill, rob the poor,
think yourself strong;
your four days reign
will end before long;
you will soon be put
where you belong.

The Boatman's ferry is full
each time it crosses.
Bulleh, you old sinner,
reckon your losses.

Where is your home?
Whence did you come?
Where do you go?

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.47. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

Name your dwelling — where did you come from, where must you go?
The station you take pride in
will not go along with you.
You do violence and torment people;
you have made plunder your trade.
After a few days' merriment
in the end you must rise and go.
Come, let us dwell in the City of the Silent,
where whole kingdoms lie contained.
Boatload after boatload the mighty ferryman carries across —
Azrael, the Angel of Death, the boatman.
Of all of these, Bulleh,
he is the oldest sinner.
Name your dwelling — where did you come from, where must you go?
