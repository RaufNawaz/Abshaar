# Original

آئی رُت شگوفیاں والی، چڑیاں چُگن آئیاں
اِکناں نوں چُریاں پھڑ کھاہدا، اِکناں پھائیاں لائیاں
اِکناں آس مُڑن دی آہے، اِک سیخ کباب چڑھائیاں
بلھے شاہ! کیہ وَس اُنھاں جو مار تقدیر پھسائیاں

# Transliteration

Aai rut shagufiyan wali, chiryan chugan aaiyan
Iknaan noon churiyaan phar khaahda, iknaan phaaiyan laaiyan
Iknaan aas muran di aahe, ik seekh kabaab charhaaiyan
Bulhe Shah! keeh vas unhaan jo maar taqdeer phasaaiyan

# Literal Translation

On the ripening corn, the birds descended.
Some were netted, some the falcons ended;
some turned back, some were turned on spits.
Who can escape what the Lord intended?

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.45. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

The season of blossoms has come; the birds came down to feed.
Some were seized and eaten, for some the snares were laid;
some still hope to turn back, some are skewered as kebab.
Bulleh Shah, what power have they whom fate has struck and ensnared?
