# Original

کیسی توبہ

کیسی توبہ ہے ایہ توبہ، ایسی توبہ نہ کر یار

مُونہوں توبہ، دِلوں نہ کردا، اِس توبہ تھِیں ترک نہ پھڑدا
کِس غفلت نے پایو پردا، تینوں بخشے کیوں غفار

سانوِیں دے کے لَؤں سوائے، ڈُہڑیاں اُتّے بازی لائے
مُسلمانی اوہ کِتّھوں پائے، جس دا ہووے ایہ کِردار

جِت نہ جانا اُتّے جاویں، حتیٰ بیگانہ ٹھمکر کھاویں
ٹھوڑ کِتاباں سِر تے چاویں، ہووے کِہ تیرا اعتبار

ظالِم ظُلموں ناہیں ڈردے، اپنی کیتیوں آپے مردے
ناہیں خوف خُدا دا کردے، ایتّے اُتّے ہوون خوار

کیسی توبہ ہے ایہ توبہ، ایسی نہ کر یار

# Transliteration

Kaisi tauba

Kaisi tauba hai eh tauba, aisi tauba na kar yaar

Moonhon tauba, dilon na karda, is tauba theen tark na phaṛda
Kis ghaflat ne paayo parda, tainu bakhshe kyun Ghaffaar

Saanveen de ke laun savaaye, ḍuhṛiyaan utte baazi laaye
Musalmaani oh kitthon paaye, jis da hove eh kirdaar

Jit na jaana utte jaaven, hataa begaana ṭhamkar khaaven
Thoṛ kitaaban sir te chaaven, hove keh tera aitbaar

Zaalim zulmon naaheen ḍarde, apni keetion aape marde
Naaheen khauf Khuda da karde, ette utte hovan khwaar

Kaisi tauba hai eh tauba, aisi na kar yaar

# Literal Translation

What sort of penitence is this?

That sits on the lips, but has no place in the heart,
and yet expects to be granted bliss;

whose name is Muslim, but whose furtive eyes
look for lacunas; whose ambitions hiss;

whose heart is absent when the hand is placed
on the Holy Book in solemn promise.

O ruthless ones, the trap you set for others,
is set for you. Be sure it will not miss.

What sort of penitence is this?

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.139. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._

# AI Translation

What kind of repentance is this repentance? — do not repent like this, friend.

Repentance by the mouth, not from the heart; from such repentance he renounces nothing;
what heedlessness has drawn its veil over you — why should the Forgiver forgive you?

[Giving little and taking more, staking everything on a gamble];
how would he ever attain true Islam, whose conduct is such as this?

[You go where you should not, you take a stranger's blows];
you set the holy books on your head [and swear] — yet what trust can be placed in you?

The tyrants have no fear of their tyranny; they die by their own doings;
they hold no fear of God — here and hereafter they are brought to disgrace.

What kind of repentance is this repentance? — do not repent like this, friend.
