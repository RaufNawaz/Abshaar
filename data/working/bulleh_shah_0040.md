# Original

کر کَتّن وَل دھیان کُڑے

کر کَتّن وَل دھیان کُڑے!

نِت مَتّیں دیندی ما، دِھیّا!
کیوں پھِرنی ایس اَینویں آ، دِھیّا!
نہ شرم حیا نوں گوا، دِھیّا
توں کدرے تاں سمجھ ندان کُڑے
کر کَتّن وَل دھیان کُڑے

نِت مَتّیں دیاں دِیاں وَلّی نوں
اِس بھولی، کملی، جھلّی نوں
بد پَؤسی وخت اِکلّی نوں
تد تھلا! کرسی جان کُڑے
کر کَتّن وَل دھیان کُڑے

اَج گھر وچ نویں کپاہ کُڑے
توں جھب جھب ویلنا ڈاہ کُڑے
توں ویل، پنجاؤن جاہ کُڑے
پھیر کل نہ تیرا جان کُڑے
کر کَتّن وَل دھیان کُڑے

ایہہ پیکا راج دِن چار کُڑے
نہ کھیڈو کھیڈ گزار کُڑے
نہ وہلی رَوہ کر کار کُڑے
گھر بار نہ کر ویران کُڑے
کر کَتّن وَل دھیان کُڑے

توں سدا نہ پیکے رہنا اے
نہ پاس اُمّڑی دے بہنا اے
بُھلّا! انت وچھوڑا سہنا اے
وت پَئیں گی سُست بِنان کُڑے
کر کَتّن وَل دھیان کُڑے

کت لَے نی بُجھ، کتا لَے نی
ہُن تانی تند اُنا لَے نی
توں اپنا داج رنگا لَے نی
توں تند ہوسیں پُر دھیان کُڑے
کر کَتّن وَل دھیان کُڑے

کر گُمان نہ حُسن جوانی دا
پردیس نہ رہن بیلانی دا
اِس دُنیا جھوٹھی فانی دا
نہ رہسی نام نشان کُڑے
کر کَتّن وَل دھیان کُڑے

اِک اَوکھا ویلا آوے گا
سب ساک سَئیں بھَج جاوے گا
کر مدد پار لنگھا وے گا
اوہ بُلھے دا سلطان کُڑے
کر کَتّن وَل دھیان کُڑے

Kar kattan val dhyaan kuṛe

Kar kattan val dhyaan kuṛe!

Nit matteen dendi maa, dhiya!
Kyun phirni es ainven aa, dhiya!
Na sharam haya nu gua, dhiya
Toon kadre taan samajh nadaan kuṛe
Kar kattan val dhyaan kuṛe

Nit matteen diyaan diyaan valli nu
Is bholi, kamli, jhalli nu
Bad paosi vakht ikalli nu
Tad thala! karsi jaan kuṛe
Kar kattan val dhyaan kuṛe

Aj ghar vich naveen kapaah kuṛe
Toon jhab jhab velna ḍaah kuṛe
Toon vel, panjaaon jaah kuṛe
Pher kal na tera jaan kuṛe
Kar kattan val dhyaan kuṛe

Eh peeka raj din chaar kuṛe
Na kheḍo kheḍ guzaar kuṛe
Na vehli roh, kar kaar kuṛe
Ghar baar na kar veeraan kuṛe
Kar kattan val dhyaan kuṛe

Toon sada na peeke rehna e
Na paas ummṛi de behna e
Bhulla! ant vichhoṛa sehna e
Vat paeen gi sust binaan kuṛe
Kar kattan val dhyaan kuṛe

Kat lai ni bujh, kata lai ni
Hun taani tand una lai ni
Toon apna daaj ranga lai ni
Toon tand hoseen pur dhyaan kuṛe
Kar kattan val dhyaan kuṛe

Kar gumaan na husn javaani da
Pardes na rehan bilaani da
Is dunya jhooṭhi faani da
Na rahsi naam nishaan kuṛe
Kar kattan val dhyaan kuṛe

Ik aukha vela aave ga
Sab saak saeen bhaj jaave ga
Kar madad paar langhaa ve ga
Oh Bulhe da Sultan kuṛe
Kar kattan val dhyaan kuṛe

# Literal Translation

Lass, look to your spinning.

Mother scolds you every day,
but your mind is far away;
you keep modesty at bay;
when will you understand?
    Lass, look to your spinning.

So much advice I hurl
each day at this silly girl;
she will be in a whirl
when bad times are at hand.
    Lass, look to your spinning.

The new cotton crop is in.
Take it for scouring, and then
sit down to spin and spin,
or spinning time will be gone.
    Lass, look to your spinning.

Here you do not belong,
so now switch off this song,
or you will lose the chance
to set up house on your own.
    Lass, look to your spinning.

What you get under this roof
topped with a fond mother's love,
will be lost soon enough.
A tough mother-in-law waits.
    Lass, look to your spinning.

The sun is high. Attend
to your chores, and send
the dowry to the dyer, then
you'll have earned each one's respect.
    Lass, look to your spinning.

You think that you are fair,
take pride in your silken hair,
but who will heed it there?
These will be gone in a trice.
    Lass, look to your spinning.

A time is coming soon
when you'll be left alone,
then help there will be none
till you call on Bulleh's guide.
    Lass, look to your spinning.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), pp.131+133+135. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training. Rafat renders "Bulhe da Sultan" (the murshid/king) as "Bulleh's guide."_

# AI Translation

Turn your mind to your spinning, girl.

Your mother counsels you every day, daughter!
Why do you drift about so aimlessly, daughter!
Do not throw away your modesty, daughter —
when will you ever understand, foolish girl?
Turn your mind to your spinning, girl.

Daily I give my counsel to her,
to this simple, dazed, half-mad girl;
hard times will fall upon the lonely one —
only then, poor thing, will she realize, girl.
Turn your mind to your spinning, girl.

Today there is fresh cotton in the house, girl;
quickly set up the cotton-gin, girl;
gin it, then go and card it, girl,
or you will have no rest tomorrow, girl.
Turn your mind to your spinning, girl.

This reign in your parents' home is but four days, girl;
do not fritter it away in play, girl;
do not sit idle — get to your work, girl;
do not let your household fall to ruin, girl.
Turn your mind to your spinning, girl.

You will not stay in your parents' home forever;
you will not always sit beside your mother;
foolish one, in the end you must bear the parting;
then you will be left helpless, girl.
Turn your mind to your spinning, girl.

Spin, understand it, get it spun;
now set up the warp and the thread;
get your own dowry dyed, girl;
keep your yarn with care, girl.
Turn your mind to your spinning, girl.

Take no pride in beauty and youth;
nor in the roaming of a far-off land;
this world is false and perishable —
not a name, not a trace of it will remain, girl.
Turn your mind to your spinning, girl.

A hard hour is going to come;
all your kin and friends will flee;
the one who will help and ferry you across —
that is Bulleh's Sultan (his master), girl.
Turn your mind to your spinning, girl.
