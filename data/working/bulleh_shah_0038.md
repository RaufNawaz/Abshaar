# Original

گل سمجھ لئی تے رَولا کیہ

گل سمجھ لئی تے رَولا کیہ
ایہ رام، رحیم تے مَولا کیہ؟

# Transliteration

Gal samajh lai te raula keh
Eh Ram, Raheem te Maula keh?

# Literal Translation

Having understood, why heed the storm
over such names as Allah and Ram.

_Reference translation by Taufiq Rafat, "Bulleh Shah: A Selection" (Vanguard, 1982), p.127. COPYRIGHTED — private reference only; do NOT publish or use for training._
